# Pain-Driven Prioritization: "Walking the Walk of Pain"

## Philosophy
Rather than speculatively implementing all proposed UX features upfront, OpenSpec Dashboard feature development is driven by **actual human pain points** encountered during real-world workflow usage.

Our comprehensive **UX Innovation Catalog (28 Ideas Across 7 Pillars)** serves as our indexed **"Idea Bank"**.

## Workflow Protocol

1. **User Reports Pain Point**: The user describes a specific friction, fatigue, or confusion point experienced during LLM artifact generation or review.
2. **Consult the Idea Bank**: The agent consults the master research artifacts stored in `<appDataDir>/brain/<conversation-id>/ux_innovation_ideas_catalog.md` and `openspec_ux_transformation_blueprint.md`.
3. **RICE & Pain-Matching Session**:
   - Match the user's pain point directly to 1-3 targeted solution paradigms in the Idea Bank.
   - Evaluate using RICE scoring (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort).
   - Propose the exact feature(s) from the bank that solve that specific pain point.
4. **Targeted Implementation**: Implement and verify only the approved, pain-relieving feature.
